##### Trust that drives data

Research data is a shared resource. It is built on the trust of people who participate in studies, and it deserves the same respect when it is shared. RDDA is our answer to the question of how social and democratic research can use its data securely, ethically and collaboratively.

##### What RDDA does

RDDA is a platform that supports the entire lifecycle of research data: from secure collection through automated anonymisation to citable publication. You don’t need specialised expertise in data protection technology to share your data responsibly. The platform guides you through every step and documents every decision in a transparent manner.

We remove direct identifiers before data leaves our secure zone. We apply mathematically verified methods, such as k-anonymity, L-diversity, T-closeness and differential privacy, and support the process with a consulting layer that helps you visualise the balance between security and data quality.

##### For research: citability rather than extra work

Data sharing is a scientific achievement. That’s why every published dataset on RDDA receives a DOI. It thus becomes an independent, citable publication that can be referenced just as precisely as a scholarly article. Your work gains visibility without requiring any additional effort on your part.

In the searchable catalog, you’ll find anonymised datasets with complete metadata. You can combine existing projects, derive new research questions from them and trace the entire provenance chain of each data instance. Every combination passes through the anonymisation process again.

![Security without any detours - placeholder image](/images/filler_21.webp)

##### For you as a researcher: Security without any detours

We’ve built RDDA so you can focus on your research. The platform accepts data directly from popular survey tools like LimeSurvey or SoSci Survey. There’s no intermediate step. An interactive wizard guides you through configuration, data matching and anonymization. At any time, you can see which options offer the best balance between data security and information content.

Once published, the dataset is immutable. The raw data is permanently deleted after the retention period expires. Only if you have a scientific reason for longer-term storage is it preserved as a living dataset.

##### For society: common good before profit

RDDA is operated by Knot RockIT PsyAnts gGmbH, a non-profit organisation dedicated to digital sovereignty. Our business model prioritises public research. Universities, public institutions and non-profit organisations pay the reduced base price. Commercial enterprises pay more, thereby subsidising access for everyone else.

Those who publish their data as open data pay the basic price. Those who use it exclusively pay a surcharge. If you decide to publish your data at a later date, we will refund half of the costs already paid. We reward transparency.

The first anonymisation analysis is free of charge for every project. This allows you to test RDDA without any obligations.

##### For the future: Certified and audited

We become certified to the following standards: ISO/IEC 27001 for information security, ISO/IEC 27018 for the protection of personal data in the cloud, ISO 9001 for quality management, and the European Commission’s Europrivacy Seal. An external data protection assessment checks our anonymisation algorithms for re-identification risks.

Every published dataset carries an automated certificate. It documents the methods used, the risk score achieved and the date of the assessment.
